# Credits

Hocus Native is an unofficial, clean-room native Windows source port. It would
not exist without the people who made the original *Hocus Pocus*.

## Original Hocus Pocus credits

The following credits are transcribed from the registered-v1.1 in-game credit
screens:

- Published by: **Apogee Software**
- Graphics: **Cygnus Multimedia Productions**
- Music: **Rob Wallace and Jeff Gatlin**
- Sound system: **Jim Dose**
- Story: **Tom Hall**
- Level design: **Andre Foucault and Mike Voss**
- Programming: **Mike Voss**

Special thanks from the original game to the entire Apogee beta test team:

- **George Broussard**
- **Jamie Cook**
